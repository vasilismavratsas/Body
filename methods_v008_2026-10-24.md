## 1. Abstract / Overview

Body is an alpha app. It colors 8 body parts (arms, shoulders, chest, upper_back, core, lower_back, hips_glutes, legs) by how much muscular work the user did that day, shown as a multiple of the work on an **average non-exerciser day**.

**How the work is calculated:**
- Each activity's work is its net energy: (MET − 1) × minutes, using 2024 Adult Compendium MET values.
- That work is split across the 8 parts by one share rule for every activity, posture and lift row: muscle mass × activation index on one %MVIC scale, normalized so each row sums to 1 (v0.7, ASSUMPTION). Shares therefore follow energy, not EMG prime-mover order.
- The same formula gives the baseline. The baseline now comes from IPUMS ATUS 2024 microdata: adults 18+ with 0 min in ATUS 1301 on the diary day, WT06-weighted, n = 5,973, which is 79.6% of weighted adults. It is 33.20 MET-h/day gross and 9.20 net. **This retires the v001 15+ all-persons stand-in.**

**How the multiple and color are computed:**
- The multiple is regularized with an additive offset: $M_p=(W_p+F_p)/(B_p+F_p)$.
- A Monte Carlo simulation (N = 4000) carries MET, share and baseline-mean uncertainty. The app reports the median and 89% interval.
- Parts are colored by bands with edges 1.15 / 1.4 / 1.8, and a continuous ramp reaches full red at 2.5×.
- An optional **typicality percentile** shows where the user's day falls among individual non-exerciser days.
- v0.7 adds a 25-activity catalog, weightlifting as a parent with 14 child lifts at 3 intensities, a lowN participation label, pRed = P(M ≥ 1.8), and a `saturated` flag (median ≥ 2.5, shown as ">2.5×").

**Evidence is still thin.** 307 of 336 share cells are ASSUMPTION (288 of the 312 activity and lift cells; §5). Results are illustrative.

## 5. Activation / share estimation

**Share rule (v0.7, spec §11; ASSUMPTION, Body decision 2026-10-24).** The same rule applies to every activity, posture and lift row, with no special case for EMG-cited cells:

$$\bar s_{a,p}=\frac{m_p I'_{a,p}}{\sum_q m_q I'_{a,q}},\qquad I'_{a,p}=\begin{cases}2\,I_{a,p} & \text{cell status ASSUMPTION}\\ I_{a,p} & \text{cited or stand-in}\end{cases}$$

- **One scale.** Cited and stand-in indices are %MVIC as published. Assumed indices use the Researcher scale: prime 50 / secondary 20 / stabilizer 10 / other 2, with activity rows up to 50. They are rescaled ×2 onto %MVIC (100 / 40 / 20 / 4); this is option B. The factor 2 (prime mover ≈ 100 %MVIC) is an ASSUMPTION (model.R A$assumed_index_scale = 2).
- **Stored shares are only checked.** The Researcher's stored `share` column is used only for the sum-to-1 check. Shares are recomputed from index × mass.
- **Why mass × activation.** The metric is energy. A large part doing moderate work can spend more than a small part working near maximum, so share order need not match EMG prime-mover order. For example (check_bands_v07_output.txt §1, rule B):
  - hip_thrust: legs 0.680 vs hips_glutes 0.252
  - bench_press: arms 0.450 vs chest 0.212
- **Colour still follows the prime mover.** Colour comes from each part's multiple against its own baseline, which restores the intuitive order. demo_output_v7.txt: hip thrust hips_glutes 1.39× ≥ legs 1.22×; bench chest 3.27× ≥ arms 1.73×. data-model.md v0.7 adds sanity tests for these orderings, and the demo meets all five: curls arms 2.11 ≥ legs 1.01; squat legs 1.39 ≥ arms 1.00; swimming shoulders 2.63 ≥ hips_glutes 1.08.
- **Sensitivity only (not adopted).** Rule A drops mass weighting for rows with EMG-cited cells. Rule C uses %MVIC directly with no mass, for every row. Table: check_bands_v07_output.txt §1.
- Mass fractions: muscle_mass_8part_v1.csv, average row. Arms 0.148, shoulders 0.0634, chest 0.0528, upper_back 0.074, core 0.0423, lower_back 0.0423, hips_glutes 0.1443, legs 0.4329.
- These rest on assumed sub-splits: legs/hips 0.75/0.25, arms/shoulders 0.70/0.30, trunk 0.25/0.35/0.20/0.20, and a 50/50 sex mix.

## 6. Mathematical model

### 6.1 Net energy

$$\text{net}_a=\begin{cases}0 & \mathrm{MET}_a^{\text{nom}}\le 1\\ \max(\mathrm{MET}_a^{(i)}-1,0) & \text{otherwise}\end{cases},\qquad \mathrm{MET}_a^{(i)}\sim\mathrm{LogNormal}(\log \mathrm{MET}_a^{\text{nom}},\ \sigma),\ \ \sigma=\sqrt{\log(1+\mathrm{CV}^2)}=0.198.$$

- Activities at rest (sleep, TV, sitting codes, MET 1.0) get **net = 0 exactly and no MET noise**. This fixes the v001 artifact, where noise around MET = 1 produced positive "rest work".
- The Compendium value is the median of the draw.
- The sdlog conversion replaces v001's use of CV = 0.20 directly as sdlog.

### 6.2 Work, baseline, offset, multiple

$$W_p=\sum_a \text{net}_a\,t_a^{\text{user}}\,s_{a,p},\qquad B_p=\sum_a \text{net}_a\,t_a^{\text{base}}\,s_{a,p}$$

$$F_p=\varphi\,m_p\sum_q B_q,\qquad M_p=\frac{W_p+F_p}{B_p+F_p},\quad \varphi=0.25.$$

- **Additive-offset floor.** $F_p=\varphi\times m_p\times\sum_q B_q$, where $m_p$ is the part's muscle-mass fraction (muscle_mass_8part_v1.csv, average row) and $\sum_q B_q$ is the total baseline net work across all 8 parts in the same draw. $\varphi=0.25$ is an ASSUMPTION.
- $F_p$ is added to **both** numerator and denominator. It acts as a pseudo-count, a regularizing prior that shrinks parts with low work toward 1×.
- It replaces v001's $W_p/\max(B_p,\text{floor}_p)$. There is no max().
- **smallBaseline** means $F_p>B_p$, i.e. the offset outweighs the part's own baseline work (spec.md §5, data-model.md core flags). model.R reports `offset_gt_B`, the share of draws in which this happens.
- $W_p$ and $B_p$ use the **same** draw. The user's day is that draw's mean baseline day with TV minutes replaced by the logged exercise. A day identical to baseline therefore gives exactly 1× for any φ; demo sedentary: all parts 1 [1, 1].

### 6.3 Baseline minutes uncertainty (v0.6 reference = average day)

Per draw, each group's mean minutes follow

$$t_g\sim\mathrm{Gamma}\big(\text{mean}=\bar t_g,\ \text{SD}=SE_g\big),\quad SE_g=\frac{SD_g}{\sqrt{n/\text{deff}}},\ n=5973,\ \text{deff}=1.5,$$

and the day is rescaled to 1440 min.

- **Minutes rescale:** in v0.4, the v1 table summed to 1440.6, so it was rescaled proportionally to 1440. Each draw is still renormalized. With v2 data, the nominal sum is already 1440.0.
- **History:** this replaces the v001 CV 0.50 ASSUMPTION. It also replaces v0.5's zero-inflated individual-day draws for M (math_v06_1 supersedes math_v05_1). Day-to-day variation is not uncertainty about the average, so it is excluded from M.

### 6.4 Typicality percentile (optional output, math_v06_2)

**Simulating individual days.** In each draw, the model also simulates one individual non-exerciser day using the same METs and shares. Each group is drawn from a zero-inflated Gamma:

$$t_g=\begin{cases}0 & \text{w.p. } 1-\pi_g\\ \mathrm{Gamma}(\mu_c,\ s_c) & \text{w.p. } \pi_g\end{cases},\quad \mu_c=\bar t_g/\pi_g,\quad s_c^2=\frac{SD_g^2+\bar t_g^2}{\pi_g}-\mu_c^2$$

- Groups are drawn independently (ASSUMPTION).
- The day is then rescaled to 1440.

**Definition.** $\text{typ}_p$ = the % of simulated individual days whose part-$p$ work is below the user's median $W_p$. It is not used in M or in the color.

**Model spread vs survey spread** (spec.md §10). These are two different quantities:
- **Survey spread (data, cited):** per-person gross MET-h/day from nonexerciser_baseline_v2.csv (IPUMS ATUS 2024, 18+, WT06): SD 4.40, p10 28.22, p50 32.60, p90 38.80.
- **Model spread (simulated):** the zero-inflated Gamma simulation of individual non-exerciser days.
  - model.R v0.7 with seed 2026 (spec §10; demo_output_v7.txt line 40): SD 4.16, p10 27.91, p50 32.34, p90 38.16.
  - v0.6.1 gave 4.13 / 28.05 / 32.15 / 38.35. The change comes from the longer random stream, which now covers more activities.
  - Across 8 other seeds in v0.6.1 (4000 draws each): SD 4.06–4.25, p10 27.90–28.12, p90 37.99–38.35.
  - It runs about 6% narrower than the survey because activity groups are drawn independently.
- The R seed-2026 values are **reference only**, not a test target.

**Test tolerances** (spec.md §10; any seed, nDraws ≥ 4000):
1. **Regression test** (HARD, any seed, any implementation): SD in [3.90, 4.40], p10 in [27.6, 28.4], p90 in [37.6, 38.8]. Demo: PASS.
2. **Survey validity test** (HARD): $|SD_{sim}-4.40|/4.40\le0.10$, $|p10_{sim}-28.22|\le1.0$, $|p90_{sim}-38.80|\le1.0$ MET-h/day. Seed 2026 gives 0.055, 0.31 and 0.64 (my arithmetic from the demo values): PASS.

- **R golden tolerance:** GOLDEN_TOL (spec §10) is ±0.01 on every median, lo89 and hi89 in demo_output_v7.txt, for same-seed R runs only. The TS port uses tests 1–3 instead.

### 6.5 Summaries, color, flags

- **Summaries:** median and 89% interval (5.5%, 94.5%) of $M_p$.
- **Bands** (model.R A7, spec.md §5 and §16 P1; ASSUMPTION, final for alpha): green < 1.15 ≤ yellow-green < 1.4 ≤ orange < 1.8 ≤ red.
- **Color ramp:**
  $$x=\min\!\Big(\frac{\max(\log_2\tilde M_p,0)}{\log_2 2.5},1\Big),$$
  mapped linearly #2E7D32 → #FBC02D → #C62828.
- **straddlesCutoff** (v0.6.1, same in model.R, spec.md and data-model.md): the 89% interval spans **≥ 2** band edges, counting edges $e$ with $lo_{89}<e\le hi_{89}$.
- **highUncertainty:** $\log_2(hi_{89}/lo_{89})>2$.
- **smallBaseline:** $F_p>B_p$. model.R reports offset_gt_B, the share of draws in which this holds.
- **referenceIsAverage:** always shown.
- **pRed** (v0.7, diagnostic): P(M ≥ 1.8) across draws.
- **saturated** (v0.7, spec §16 P2 adopted; model.R A12): median ≥ M_cap = 2.5. The UI shows the value as ">2.5×", because colour is already full red there and "how far above red" would otherwise be invisible.
- **lowN** (v0.7): a per-activity data-quality label (§3.1). It is not a per-part flag and does not change M.

## 7. Limitations

1. **Shares are mostly assumed.** 307 of 336 cells are ASSUMPTION, including 105 of 112 lift cells, and every round-2 sport except swimming's three cells is ASSUMPTION.
2. **The share rule is a modelling choice, not a physiological finding** (methods_notes "Limitation (Mathematician, v0.7)").
   - Shares are active mass × activation on one %MVIC scale, with assumed indices rescaled ×2 (ASSUMPTION), for every row.
   - Share order therefore follows energy, not EMG prime-mover order (hip thrust legs 0.68 vs glutes 0.25; bench arms 0.45 vs chest 0.21).
   - %MVIC is not a measure of energy. Using it as a per-mass energy-rate proxy is an assumption, and mixing published %MVIC with rescaled guesses is cruder still.
   - The cited heavy-load values probably over-weight the cited parts.
   - Rules A and C are sensitivity analyses only.
3. **Small parts saturate quickly** (spec §17; methods_notes item 5).
   - Chest, core, shoulders and upper_back have small sedentary baselines, so a heavy isolated lift or swimming turns them red within about 20–45 min, often above the 2.5× cap.
   - This is expected under the definition. The `saturated` flag shows ">2.5×", and φ stays 0.25.
4. **Compendium MET ranges are treated as 89% intervals** (ASSUMPTION). A range across codes is not a sampling distribution. For activities whose default code sits at an edge of the range, the lognormal centred on the default doesn't match the range.
5. **lowN participation.** Five activities rest on fewer than 10 ATUS respondents. Diary-day rates are not annual participation rates (method_log r2_7). Proxies stand in for swimming, tennis, elliptical and rowing.
6. **yard_work is provisional.** Its shares copy gardening, pending the Researcher's delivery.
7. **Mass fractions** rely on DXA lean mass (not muscle) and on assumed sub-splits.
8. **The offset φ = 0.25 controls the trunk parts.** Chest, core and upper_back are offset-dominated, so their multiples stay near 1× for whole-body activity. Under direct loading they still go red (limitation 3).
9. **Groups are drawn independently**, with deff 1.5 assumed and no replicate weights. The typicality spread is slightly too narrow.
10. **Group METs come from one Compendium code per ATUS group.** Work is coded as desk work (11580, 1.5), and travel is folded into parent categories.
11. **METs assume a standard resting rate.** Differences in body mass, fitness and occupation are not captured. The reference is the average non-exerciser, not the user.
12. **The user day is built by swapping TV minutes** (a scenario ASSUMPTION). Each user activity is mapped to a single Compendium code, and each lift to an intensity code (ASSUMPTION).
13. **The non-exerciser definition uses a single diary day** (0 min of 1301 that day). It is not a measure of habitual inactivity.
